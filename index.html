Mapping Health and Ageing in India: Early Findings from BHARAT

What initial data from the BHARAT Study reveal about diabetes, iron, vitamins, inflammation and ageing, drawing on blood tests, clinical and questionnaire data, and a pilot plasma proteome study.

Amruth D Bhat · Longevity India, IISc Bangalore · September 2026
Data: BHARAT Study (Longevity India, IISc Bangalore), baseline visit. Figures are aggregate estimates; no individual-level data are published. Analyses in R 4.5 (mgcv GAMMs with laboratory and site random intercepts). Thresholds: American Diabetes Association. Early findings, not peer reviewed.
